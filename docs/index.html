Our products

The Memo apps

Two free Android apps made to work together. MemoCam takes the photo and saves the details of the moment. MemoShare organises those photos and shares them — without the cloud.

MemoCam

Simple camera. Smart memory.

A camera that works the way you expect. Every photo also saves the place, the weather, the time and the direction you were facing — on the photo and inside the file.

  • Live GPS, city, temperature, altitude and compass on every shot
  • Distance from home, two clocks and a trip day counter
  • Photos and video, five zoom levels, speedometer gauge
Learn more Google Play

MemoShare

Stamp many photos. Share offline.

A photo gallery with helpers that save you hours. Add the same information to a whole album in one tap, then share it over your own Wi-Fi with a QR code.

  • Stamp date, place and weather on many photos at once
  • ShareZone — friends scan a code and download, no app needed
  • Trip maps, filters, crop and beautify
Learn more Google Play

How they fit together

Capture in the moment. Share afterwards.

The two apps split the work on purpose. MemoCam stays fast and simple, because a camera should never make you wait. MemoShare handles everything that happens after — choosing, stamping, organising and sending.

  • MemoCam is dark — made for the viewfinder, day or night.
  • MemoShare is light — made for browsing and editing your photos.
  • They share the same style, so a photo looks the same in both.

About

A small studio making small, useful software

Cekli is an independent software studio. We make medium-sized software, mostly mobile apps, and we keep them simple on purpose. An app should do one job well, start quickly, and not ask for anything it does not need.

Right now our work is focused on the Memo apps for Android. They are free, they are built with .NET MAUI, and they are tested on real phones over many months before anything ships.
