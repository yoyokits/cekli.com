The three helpers

A simple gallery that saves you hours

MemoShare looks and feels like a normal photo gallery. Big photos, a clear list, nothing new to learn. But it does three things no normal gallery does — all without a cloud, a sign-in, or an internet connection.

1. Stamp many photos at once

Choose up to 50 photos, pick a style, and add the date, the place, the trip name and the weather to all of them in one tap. Your old photos suddenly look like part of the same trip. The original files are not changed.

2. Share over your own Wi-Fi

Tap Share and a QR code appears. Friends in the same room scan it, a clean gallery opens in their browser, and they download what they want. Nothing is uploaded. When you close MemoShare, the gallery is gone.

3. Big photos you can read

Most galleries show tiny photos in a small grid, so the stamp becomes unreadable. MemoShare shows big photos, so you can always read the date, the city and the GPS. Filter by date, place or trip.

More in the app

Everything that happens after the photo

  • Trip view — pick two dates and MemoShare draws your route on a world map with the total distance, ready to share as one picture.
  • Simple edits — crop, filters, brightness, contrast, colour and a beautify tool. The original photo is always kept.
  • Easy export — save a full album as a ZIP file, save a single photo, or use the normal Android share button.
  • Works with MemoCam — photos taken in MemoCam arrive with their details already attached, and are marked in the list.
